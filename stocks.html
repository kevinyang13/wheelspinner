📖 How to play

Everyone starts with the same money and trades on the one screen — tap a name to take the controls, buy and sell in any order, as often as you like. Prices only move when somebody runs the market, which ends the round and counts up everyone's net worth.

  • Buy / sell shares at the price on the board.
  • Sell a put — you promise to buy 10 shares at the strike price. You get paid a premium now, and that much cash is held aside. If the price ends up below the strike, you must buy at the strike.
  • Sell a covered call — you own 10 shares and promise to sell them at the strike. You get paid a premium now and the shares are locked. If the price ends up above the strike, they are sold at the strike.
  • Options last one round. Every round each stock jumps 30%–50% up or down, so nothing is safe.
  • The game ends when everybody says they're done, or when someone is wiped out.
